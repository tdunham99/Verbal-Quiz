# Boba Milk Tea Recipe Quiz

<ins>Background</ins>

I worked at a Tea Shop back when I was a freshman in college. Being closely located to a high-school, this was a fast paced environment that required me to make orders as efficiently as I could. 

In order to help me memorize the many recipes for milk teas that customers could possibly order, I developed a "pop quiz" written in Java that provides real-time feedback on your answers.

<ins>Ingredients [units of measurements]:</ins>
- Liquid sugar [fl oz]
- Shaker size [cc or cubic centimeters]
- Water [cc]
- Flavored Syrup [oz]

## Quiz Format

1. You will be prompted a list of drinks that will be on the quiz (MT = Milk Tea, GT = Green Tea):

>  1. Grass Jelly Roasted MT   2. Roasted MT   3. Tea Bar MT   4. Mango GT   5. Kumquat GT   6. Jasmine MT   7. Oolong MT

_The "orders" can either come in Regular or Large size, which affects how much sugar, water, syrup, and shaker size to use._

2. The program randomizes these orders for a total of seven questions, each answer is tallied towards the final score at the end of the quiz.

3. In each question the user has to provide the amount of sugar, shaker size, amount of flavored syrup, and amount of water to use. 

4. Finally, the user is presented with their final score and is asked if they want to continue playing, or not.

## How to run

Just download the files and run Game.java in your favorite IDE. I have not developed a stand-alone application that can run this project, nor do I plan to. I forgot the recipes that this quiz depends on
